# /sys/ames

Other ships live here. `/sys/ames/ships/<ship>` is made the first time a
ship sends anything, with a `ship.sig` its darts arrive from and a weir
on the directory; `/sys/ames/usergroups` holds the groups that decide
that weir; `/sys/ames/registry` is the one service grub, taking the
registry actions as pokes. Our own ship has a directory too, with no
weir. [Usergroups](#usergroups.md) is the weir side and
[Cross-ship](#cross-ship.md) the wire.
