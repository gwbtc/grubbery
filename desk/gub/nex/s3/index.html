A mount is a path of a bucket mirrored into the namespace at /apps/s3/mounts/<bucket>/<path>: a folder, a single file, or the whole bucket. Pull downloads it; Push uploads it back; Open browses the local copy.

About this nexus

This nexus is the ship's one door to S3-compatible buckets. Apps that use it never hold the credentials or reach the internet; they poke a call and read the result, and every operation is logged to its caller. A bucket meets the namespace through mounts: a named folder mirrored as a directory, pulled from the bucket and pushed back to it.
