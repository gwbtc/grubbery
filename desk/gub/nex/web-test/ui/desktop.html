a desktop, apparently

Two components: <float-window> and <desk-bar>. The bar found every window on this page by itself — no registration, no wiring.

  • drag the titlebar; resize any edge or corner
  • click a window (or its taskbar button) to foreground it
  • double-click a titlebar to shade it to just the bar
  • minimize hides it; its taskbar button dims — click to restore
  • reload — persisted windows come back exactly where you left them
  • snap — shove a drag against the left/right edge for a half, a corner for a quarter, the top for full screen; drag away to unsnap

The start menu is bottom-left. The + spawns throwaway windows.
