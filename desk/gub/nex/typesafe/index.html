About this nexus

This nexus is the ship's door to TypeSafe AI's Jev, a System One model: it answers typed questions (choice, score, yes/no) with calibrated probabilities instead of generating text. Apps that go through it never hold the key or reach the internet; they are granted this nexus's road, and every call is metered to its caller. Jev decides, code computes.
